ARM-04 · Sitemap and B2B funnels

Every road ends in a quote.

The site is a funnel, not a brochure. Five buyer types enter on the Home signal path, take the shortest route to the hardware that answers their question, and finish at the RFQ wizard or a measurable micro-conversion.

2 · Funnel lanes

One lane per persona (SPEC §4). Each box names the screen and the question in the buyer's head. Every lane ends in a dark box: a conversion.

Automation / controls engineerNeeds exact I/O, protocols, power, pinout, datasheet
  1. H1 HeroSee a real IA015 render. "Is this real hardware?"
  2. H3 Board explorerHover the parts. "What's on the board?"
  3. /products/:codeDatasheet table, pinout. "Exact I/O, power, mounting?"
  4. MicroView datasheet (GitHub) · copy MQTT snippet. "Can I integrate it?"
  5. ConversionRequest sample. need=sample&product=ia015&who=engineer
Industrial buyer / procurementNeeds reliability, who is behind it, support, a quote
  1. H1 HeroHeadline and hardware. "Who are these people?"
  2. H6 Parts drawerScan 17 boards by spec. "Which board fits?"
  3. H8 ProofRepos, reviews, orders (sourced). "Can I trust them?"
  4. MicroDownload catalogue (PDF). "Let me share this internally."
  5. ConversionRequest a quote (H9 or overlay). need=product (who chosen in step 1)
System integrator / OEMNeeds customisation, white-label, firmware changes, volume
  1. H1 / H2Meet the signal path. "Do they do protocol work?"
  2. H7 EngineeringSix PCB stages. "Can they build my board?"
  3. H6 / detailCheck a base board. "Can I start from IA013?"
  4. ConversionStart a custom design. who=oem&need=custom
Plant / facility manager"Can I monitor my old machines without replacing them?"
  1. H2 Signal pathUnderstand IIoT in 5 s. "What does it do for my cabinet?"
  2. H5 Retro to IIoTDrag silent to talking. "Do I have to replace the PLC?"
  3. H4 ConsoleFlip a relay, see data. "What would I see?"
  4. MicroWatch a third-party review (H8). "Has anyone else used it?"
  5. ConversionAssess my machines. who=plant&need=retro
Maker / developer (secondary)Needs open source, Tasmota, GitHub
  1. H8 ProofPublic repos, Tasmota docs. "Is it open?"
  2. MicroBrowse repos on GitHub. "Show me the code."
  3. /products/:codeMQTT snippet, programming notes. "Can I flash my own firmware?"
  4. ConversionRequest sample (short wizard). who=developer&need=sample&product=ba011
Micro-conversions (catalogue PDF, datasheet, MQTT copy, repos, review video) catch visitors who are not ready to ask for a quote. Each is a measurable intent signal and each leads back to the RFQ.

The RFQ is a five-step wizard, not one long form: each tap is small, the answers qualify the lead (who, need, spec, volume) and "Developer" gets a shorter path. Sales receives a structured request, not a vague "contact us" message.
On mobile the sticky bar keeps "Request quote" and "Call" under the thumb on every screen, because plant visits and shop-floor browsing happen on phones.
