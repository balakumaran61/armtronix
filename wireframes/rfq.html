    Why a wizard: five small decisions are quicker than one long form, each answer qualifies the lead (who, need, spec, volume), and Developer gets a shorter step 3. Sales receives a structured request. Pre-fill works from the URL: ?who=, ?need=, ?product=ia015,ba011, ?topic=tech.
